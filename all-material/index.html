Common Phrases

Common Phrases 1-10

  1. मुझे भूख लगी है
    mujhe bhūkh lagī hai
    I am hungry
  2. मुझे प्यास लगी है
    mujhe pyās lagī hai
    I am thirsty
  3. मुझे नींद आ रही है
    mujhe nīnd ā rahī hai
    I am sleepy
  4. मैं थका हूँ / मैं थकी हूँ
    maiṁ thakā hūṁ / maiṁ thakī hūṁ
    I am tired (masculine / feminine)
  5. मेरा पेट भर गया
    merā peṭ bhar gayā
    My stomach is full / I am full
  6. और लीजिए
    aur līji​ye
    Please take more / Have some more
  7. नहीं, बस
    nahīṁ, bas
    No, enough / No, that's enough
  8. कुछ और?
    kuch aur?
    Something more? / Anything else?
  9. आपको क्या चाहिए?
    āpko kyā cāhie?
    What do you want? / What would you like?
  10. थोड़ा पानी
    thoṛā pānī
    A little water
Nouns
  • भूख – bhūkh – hunger
  • प्यास – pyās – thirst
  • नींद – nīnd – sleep
  • पेट – peṭ – stomach
  • पानी – pānī – water
Pronouns
  • मैं – maiṁ – I
  • मुझे – mujhe – to me / me (dative/accusative)
  • मेरा – merā – my / mine (masculine object)
  • आपको – āpko – to you / you (dative/accusative, polite)
Verbs
  • होना – honā – to be
    • हूँ – hūṁ – am (first person)
    • है – hai – is/am/are
  • लगना – lagnā – to feel/seem
    • लगी है – lagī hai – feels/seems (feminine)
  • आना – ānā – to come
    • आ रही है – ā rahī hai – is coming (continuous, feminine)
  • भरना – bharnā – to fill/become full
    • भर गया – bhar gayā – became full (past perfect, masculine)
  • लेना – lenā – to take
    • लीजिए – lījiye – please take (polite imperative)
  • चाहना – cāhnā – to want
    • चाहिए – cāhiye – want/need
Others
  • थका/थकी – thakā/thakī – tired (masculine/feminine)
  • थोड़ा – thoṛā – a little/some
  • और – aur – more/additional
  • कुछ – kuch – some/something
  • क्या – kyā – what
  • नहीं – nahīṁ – no/not
  • बस – bas – enough/just/stop

Common Phrases 11-20

  1. कुछ नहीं
    kuch nahīṁ
    Nothing
  2. मेरा (खाना) हो गया
    merā (khānā) ho gayā
    I'm done (eating) / My (food) is finished
  3. हो गया
    ho gayā
    It's done / It's finished
  4. बिल दीजिए
    bil dījiye
    Please give the bill
  5. ठीक है
    ṭhīk hai
    Okay / Alright / That's fine
  6. कोई बात नहीं
    koī bāt nahīṁ
    No problem / It doesn't matter / No worries
  7. शुक्रिया
    śukriyā
    Thank you
  8. आपसे मिल कर अच्छा लगा
    āpse mil kar acchā lagā
    It was nice meeting you
  9. फिर मिलेंगे
    phir mileṅge
    See you again / We'll meet again
  10. मैं बीमार हूँ
    maiṁ bīmār hūṁ
    I am sick
Nouns
  • खाना – khānā – food
  • बिल – bil – bill
  • बात – bāt – matter / thing / talk
Pronouns
  • मैं – maiṁ – I
  • मेरा – merā – my / mine (masculine object)
  • आपसे – āpse – from you / with you (polite)
Verbs
  • होना – honā – to be
    • है – hai – is/am/are
    • हो गया – ho gayā – became/got done (past perfect, masculine)
  • देना – denā – to give
    • दीजिए – dījiye – please give (polite imperative)
  • लगना – lagnā – to feel/seem
    • लगा – lagā – felt/seemed (past tense, masculine)
  • मिलना – milnā – to meet
    • मिल कर – mil kar – having met/after meeting
    • मिलेंगे – mileṅge – will meet (future tense)
Others
  • कोई – koī – any / anyone / some (indefinite determiner)
  • ठीक – ṭhīk – okay / fine / correct
  • शुक्रिया – śukriyā – thank you (interjection)
  • अच्छा – acchā – good / nice
  • फिर – phir – again / then
  • बीमार – bīmār – sick / ill

Sentence Expansion

Set 1 - The Boy and the Mangoes

Question 1

यह लड़का क्या कर रहा है?
yah laṛkā kyā kar rahā hai?
What is this boy doing?

Answer 1

यह लड़का आम खा रहा है।
yah laṛkā ām khā rahā hai.
This boy is eating mangoes.

Question 2

वह लड़का कहाँ आम खा रहा है?
vah laṛkā kahāṁ ām khā rahā hai?
Where is that boy eating mangoes?

Answer 2

वह लड़का घर में आम खा रहा है।
vah laṛkā ghar meṁ ām khā rahā hai.
That boy is eating mangoes at home.

Question 3

वह आम कैसा है?
vah ām kaisā hai?
How is that mango?

Answer 3

वह आम बहुत मीठा और रसीला है।
vah ām bahut mīṭhā aur rasīlā hai.
That mango is very sweet and juicy.
Nouns
  • लड़का – laṛkā – boy
  • आम – ām – mango
  • घर – ghar – house/home
Verbs
  • होना – honā – to be
    • है – hai – is/am/are
  • करना – karnā – to do
    • कर रहा है – kar rahā hai – is doing (continuous, masculine)
  • खाना – khānā – to eat
    • खा रहा है – khā rahā hai – is eating (continuous, masculine)
